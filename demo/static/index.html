Architecture — three nested timescales

Why a controller — not another signal

1
Every checkpoint, ask the model early.
Inject the answer cue mid-thought; the forced trial answer costs ~3–5% extra tokens.
2
No single signal survives everywhere.
Agreement beats full thinking on GSM8K (+4.5) but collapses on MCQ (−12.3) and AIME (−24.6). Confidence is safe but barely saves. Proven across 6 benchmarks × 3 scales × 2 families.
3
Learn which signal to trust, per domain.
A slow tier cross-validates all signals on a small warm-up and deploys the cheapest one within your accuracy budget ε — provably never worse than its best member on calibration.
4
Route the model too.
The same nesting picks small vs large models per query — vanilla-level accuracy at half the compute.
